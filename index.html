The journey

From a hosting headache to a hosting company

Gen X Web Hosting was not a business plan. It was a workaround that other people kept asking to use.

2011

The problem, first-hand

While building an eCommerce platform, my team had to pick and configure a hosting server. What should have been a checkout turned into a week of research, mismatched specs and support tickets. We decided the process itself was the product worth fixing.

2013

Gen X Web Hosting goes live

The workaround became a product, run out of Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Shared Linux and Windows hosting came first, followed by reseller plans for the agencies and freelancers who were reselling us informally anyway.

2020s

Cloud, containers and automation

The stack grew to managed cPanel and Plesk hosting, WordPress, Node.js and Python environments, Node X cloud servers, dedicated hardware, business email, SSL and managed n8n automation hosting — with a team of engineers in Lucknow behind it.

Today

NiviChat, and the second problem

Hosting customers kept describing the same gap: enquiries arriving on the website and on WhatsApp at 11 p.m., with nobody to answer them. NiviChat is the answer to that — an AI chatbot that sells, supports and books appointments in the way India actually talks. Both brands now sit under Nivi Cloud Pvt. Ltd.

How I work

The same three rules, both times

Different products, identical opinions about how they should behave.

Simple by default

The interesting decisions should be yours, not the setup's. If a product needs a tutorial before it does anything useful, that is a design failure, not a documentation gap.

A human at the end of it

Automation should absorb the repetitive nine tickets out of ten and hand the tenth to a person who can actually decide something. That is how NiviChat is built, and how our hosting desk has always run.

Human handoff, both products

Built here, priced for here

Made in India, for the way Indian businesses actually buy and talk — WhatsApp first, rupee pricing, and a free tier that is genuinely usable rather than a two-week trial in disguise.
